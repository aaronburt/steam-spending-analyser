Import your purchase historyRuns in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  1. Sign in and open store.steampowered.com/account/history.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and click Load More Transactions until the list stops growing.
  3. Select the whole page with Ctrl+A (⌘+A on Mac) and copy it.
  4. Paste it into the box and click Analyse.

Works with Steam set to English. Desktop Chrome or Edge copies the table most reliably.
